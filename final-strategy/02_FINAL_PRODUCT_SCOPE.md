# Final Product Scope

## Phase 1 MVP

Phase 1 is a multi-brand AI marketing autopilot for local businesses, agencies, multi-brand operators, and done-for-you operators.

Must ship:
- Signup/login, workspace creation, brand creation, and brand switching.
- Agentic onboarding with safe website/social/competitor scan interfaces.
- Brand intelligence, marketing scores, target market report, and editable brand profile.
- 30-day content calendar with platform variants and evidence.
- Approval queue with approve, reject, regenerate, batch, and mobile-first flows.
- Media library and mock image generation, with real providers behind config.
- Manual export scheduler and typed Vista Social / Buffer skeletons.
- Safe DM/comment rule drafting only, approval required.
- Weekly report and growth opportunity mock workflows.
- Stripe billing skeleton, usage tracking, and plan limits.
- Admin dashboard for users, brands, agent runs, workflow runs, failures, and costs.
- Read-only MCP inspection endpoint.

Out of scope for Phase 1:
- Direct Meta/X/TikTok publishing as a dependency.
- Browser-bot DMs or private social automation.
- Creator autopilot and influencer marketplace.
- Vectorize semantic memory unless Phase 1 data proves the need.
