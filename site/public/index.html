Your photos in. Good reels out.

01 · How it will work

Four steps. You do the first one.

  1. 1

    Drop photos in a folder

    Phone photos and short clips go into a shared folder. No shoot day, no crew, no new app to learn.

    Working on our own brands

  2. 2

    It cuts the reels

    Short vertical reels made from your footage, not stock. Captions sit clear of the app buttons. Every cut is checked before you see it.

    Working on our own brands

  3. 3

    You say yes

    You see the week's reels in one place. Approve, swap or skip. Nothing goes out until you approve it, and it only ever posts on your accounts.

    Building

  4. 4

    The numbers come back

    Views, saves and sign-ups, each tied to the reel that earned them. So next week's reels learn from this week's.

03 · Who it's for

Places people walk into.

Cafés, salons, gyms, shops, laundromats, the repair place down the street. If customers come through your door and your phone is full of photos nobody sees, it's built for you.

Tell us what you run when you join. It helps us decide who gets the first spots.

04 · House rules

What it will never do.

  • Make up customers. No AI people posing as reviewers or fans.
  • Buy reach. No bought followers and no likes from accounts we control.
  • Post from our accounts. Your reels go on your accounts and nowhere else.
  • Post without your yes. Everything starts as a draft.
  • Hide AI. If something in a reel was generated, the reel says so.
  • Take your customer list. The numbers that come back are counts, never names or phone numbers.
