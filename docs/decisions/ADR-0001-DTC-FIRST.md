# ADR-0001: Launch DTC-first

## Status

Accepted on 2026-07-12.

## Decision

Launch for US, English-language, Shopify-first DTC/e-commerce brands with 1–5 person growth or creative teams. Optimize the first product for a founder, Head of Growth, or Performance Creative lead producing a Meta Campaign Launch Pack. Agency features wait until this workflow earns repeat paid use.

## Rationale

A single buyer, channel, asset package, and success measure makes product validation interpretable. Combining DTC and agency needs would add client hierarchies, white-labeling, multi-client reporting, approvals, and billing before proving that the core creation workflow is valuable.

## Consequences

- Briefs, terminology, onboarding, exports, pricing, and usability tests use DTC language and Meta campaign needs.
- Shopify URLs are metadata during P0; catalog import is deferred.
- Agency requests become research evidence, not launch-scope commitments.
- Reconsider only after P0/P1a evidence demonstrates paid retention or a documented failure of the DTC hypothesis.
