# ADR-0007: Build the full brand and social operations platform

## Status and evidence

Accepted September 9, 2026 by the owner's explicit implementation instruction and immediate
rebaseline selection. Source provenance and authorization are committed in WP-P3-009's owner
supersession decision; ADR-0008 defines the transition mechanism. This is a product-direction
decision, not a claim that the previous DTC hypothesis was validated or that the platform exists.

## Decision

MustBeViral serves single-brand operators and studios managing multiple client brands across local
services and e-commerce. Adopt all 65 work units from the supplied September 9 full-platform plan
into the existing roadmap, with the five golden journeys and evidence levels in quality gates.

Brand knowledge and actual media precede campaign creation. Include website/document ingestion,
persistent portfolios, client permissions and approvals, social connections and publishing,
creator operations, partnerships, engagement, analytics, learning and commercial operations.
Paid-media execution and creator payouts retain separate commercial and operational acceptance.

Preserve the V2 platform boundaries, workspace tenancy, Postgres authority, private R2, immutable
revisions, ledger and execution evidence. Reuse working mechanisms. The default product is a
durable studio/brand workflow; the graph is an advanced creative execution surface.

Evaluate Postiz, then Sendible, then direct channel adapters against documented capability,
isolation, rights, cost, scheduling and reconciliation criteria. No vendor selection, purchase,
commercial agreement or live publishing authorization is implied by this ordering.

## Authority and delivery

This decision supersedes ADR-0001 and the previous finish-sprint instruction. Existing registered
product, release, UX, architecture, terminology, roadmap and quality documents are amended in place.
No second ship-plan database is created. Product-scope tests change to enforce the accepted scope;
security, money, privacy, deployment containment and required GitHub checks/reviews remain enforced.

WP-P3-009 was superseded without passing its unfinished observation or traffic decision. Those
obligations remain release gates. W0 and subsequent local work proceed through one bounded active
packet without waiting for production traffic. New production UI follows concrete desktop/mobile
design review; external effects require their own exact resources, permissions and rollback.

## Consequences

- A complete launch-pack implementation does not establish full-platform completion.
- Owner corrections, source evidence, identity assets, rights and approvals are durable/versioned.
- Missing permissions, unavailable metrics, failed jobs and ambiguous publication remain explicit.
- Historical contracts, price versions, receipts and migrations retain their original meaning.
- Local commits are authorized for packet continuity; protected-branch review and release approval
  are separate acts. A PR author cannot be recorded as their own independent reviewer.
