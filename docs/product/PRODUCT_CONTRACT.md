# Product contract

## Customer and buyer

MustBeViral Studio launches for US, English-language, Shopify-first DTC/e-commerce brands with a 1–5 person growth or creative team. The primary buyer is a founder, Head of Growth, or Performance Creative lead managing meaningful Meta advertising spend and personally accountable for creative throughput.

Agency-specific operation is deliberately excluded until the DTC workflow earns repeat paid use.

## Job to be done

When a small growth team must launch or refresh a Meta campaign, it needs to turn approved product truth and brand constraints into multiple reviewable creative directions quickly, understand the likely execution cost before spending, preserve the relationship between every input and output, rerun only work invalidated by a change, and export a production-ready package without coordinating several disconnected tools.

The product promise is: **from a trustworthy campaign brief to a reviewable, versioned launch pack in one transparent visual workflow.**

## Initial job: Meta Campaign Launch Pack

### Required inputs

- Product packshots uploaded by the user.
- Structured product name, category, features, benefits, evidence, and approved facts.
- Offer, price presentation, urgency constraints, and destination URL metadata.
- Brand kit: logo, colors, typography, tone, visual rules, examples, and prohibited treatments.
- Target audience, awareness stage, pain points, desires, and objections.
- Required claims, prohibited claims, legal copy, and creative constraints.
- Product URL as metadata only during P0; the product does not crawl or import it.

The brief cannot execute until required fields and asset rights attestations pass validation. Generated copy may use only supplied or explicitly approved factual claims.

### Planning and selection

P0 does not ask the buyer to pick one of three unpaid directions. The Meta Campaign Launch Pack is one quoted graph: three copy angles (problem-recognition, proof-first, offer-clarity), three master stills, nine adaptations, and one motion branch. All of it is priced and run together.

1. The structured brief and brand constraints pin that pack onto an immutable canvas revision.
2. The buyer can inspect the graph before spend; editing the paid shape is not required to complete P0.
3. Selection is review: approve the concepts worth exporting after seeing composed ads.
4. The system validates the graph, produces a transparent quote, and requires explicit confirmation of the maximum charge.

### P0 tenancy and last mile

P0 has one workspace membership role: `owner`. Invites, roles, and agency client portals are out of phase.

A buyer-ready P0 demo still owes these last-mile obligations. They are named here so they cannot be forgotten, and they are implemented in a dedicated successor packet rather than invented as undocumented APIs inside a golden-run step:

- The buyer can attach their own packshot through the existing signed-upload operation.
- Live Review shows composed ads and records approval through the existing approval operation.
- Rejection is either a durable reject operation with a reason category or is not offered. A local note is not a recorded rejection.
- Export yields a private ZIP through the existing `customer_download` capability, not a receipt of a file the buyer cannot take.
- Copy presented on ads is the offer (headline, primary text, short description), not a spec dump.

### Required output bundle

The launch pack produces:

- Three master static concepts.
- Approved adaptations in 4:5 at 1080×1350, 1:1 at 1080×1080, and 9:16 at 1080×1920.
- Three copy sets, each containing primary text, headline, and description.
- One 6–10 second motion variant in 9:16.
- QA results covering required sizes, readable safe areas, brand constraints, supplied claims, prohibited claims, and missing evidence.
- Versioned preview, comparison, approval, and rejection records.
- Export bundle with deterministic names, manifest, asset files, copy, QA report, and immutable cost/lineage receipt.

Video may complete after the static pack and must appear as a clearly labeled partial branch rather than blocking reviewable static work.

## Trust contract

- Nothing presented as generated is a hidden mock or silent fallback.
- The user sees the pinned revision, model route, provider, price basis, progress, partial failures, and final receipt.
- Media is private by default and copied from temporary provider storage into private R2 immediately after verification.
- A changed input invalidates only affected descendants; approved unaffected artifacts remain inspectable.
- No run starts without explicit confirmation of an unexpired quote.
- No duplicate submission, webhook, retry, or reconciliation event can create duplicate work or money movement.
- The system does not infer permission to use unlicensed assets, unsupported claims, sensitive likenesses, or prohibited advertising content.

## Product-validation success

P0 is a go only when 20 representative briefs and 5–8 qualified users prove the technical,
usability, quality, economic, and paid-demand gates in `quality-gates`. A technically functioning
workflow is insufficient if users reject the outputs, cost exceeds the target, or no qualified DTC
customer or design partner has sanitized durable evidence that it will use staging and either
intends to pay or has agreed commercial terms. Actual payment and Stripe are not required in P0;
the qualification and commitment evidence is.
