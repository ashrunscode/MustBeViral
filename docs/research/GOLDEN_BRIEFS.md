# Golden briefs

This corpus defines 20 representative campaign briefs for the US, English-language, Shopify-first DTC launch ICP. Every brand, product, URL, asset, and item of evidence is synthetic test data. Before a run, the operator must attach the described fixture assets, confirm the recorded rights attestation, and treat the destination URL as metadata only.
