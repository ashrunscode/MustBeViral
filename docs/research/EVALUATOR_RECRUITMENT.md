# Evaluator qualification and recruitment

This document defines the D0 evaluator cohort, recruitment method, and living recruitment record. It is an informative operating record, not proof that a pending candidate is qualified or recruited. Use candidate IDs only; keep names, contact details, brand names, and scheduling links in the approved private recruiting system.

## Qualification definition

Every evaluator must satisfy all of these criteria:

1. Operates a US-based Shopify DTC brand or directly runs growth/performance creative for one.
2. The represented brand has active Meta advertising spend of at least $2,000 per month, verified by a redacted current-platform view, agency/client attestation, or equivalent private evidence.
3. Is personally accountable for creative throughput: briefing, producing, approving, or shipping paid-social creative at least monthly.
4. Currently uses at least one AI creative tool in the workflow or personally relies on a manual designer workflow. The final cohort must include at least two current AI-tool users and at least two current manual-workflow users; a mixed-workflow operator may count in only one predeclared cohort bucket.
5. Can complete an English-language remote evaluation using a real or sanitized campaign brief and can discuss current workflow time, cost, and quality tradeoffs.
6. Has no friend-of-founder conflict: no friendship, family relationship, current employment, investment relationship, or other material personal incentive that could bias product feedback.
7. Is not participating solely as an agency representative, service provider selling to the project, employee, contractor, or direct product-development contributor.

Qualification is candidate-specific. One brand cannot contribute more than one evaluator to the core five-person preference cohort. Screening evidence remains private; this file records only the decision and a non-identifying note.

## Cohort target and composition

- Recruit eight qualified evaluators when scheduling permits; five completed qualified sessions is the minimum cohort.
- Reserve the first five qualified candidates who complete sessions as the predeclared core preference cohort; report all supplementary sessions separately and do not swap participants based on sentiment.
- Maintain at least two AI-tool users and at least two manual-workflow users among the completed core cohort.
- Seek category spread across at least four DTC categories and role spread across founders, growth leads, and performance-creative leads.
- Stop counting a candidate if qualification changes before the session or the conflict screen becomes positive.

## Recruitment plan

### Sourcing channels

Use channels in this order so the cohort remains operator-led and conflict-screened:

1. Operator network: direct referrals from Shopify founders, fractional growth leads, and paid-social operators, excluding anyone with a friend-of-founder conflict.
2. Vetted DTC communities: eCommerceFuel, the DTC Newsletter community, Foxwell Founders, and relevant Shopify Community groups, subject to each community’s research and solicitation rules.
3. X DTC circles: public posts and direct outreach to operators who openly describe current Shopify and Meta responsibilities; never infer qualification from follower count.
4. Paid-research fallback: User Interviews or Respondent, with the same spend, responsibility, workflow, and conflict screens before scheduling.

Do not recruit by promising early access, favorable coverage, investment, vendor work, or a positive outcome. Referrals identify prospects only; every prospect completes the same screen.

### Outreach script

> We are evaluating a workflow that turns an approved Shopify campaign brief into a quoted, reviewable Meta launch pack. We need operators who personally own creative throughput for a US DTC brand spending at least $2,000 per month on Meta. The session is 90 minutes, remote, and paid at $200. You will work through the product without coaching and compare it with your current process. Payment is for your time, not for a favorable opinion. A short qualification screen comes first. Reply if this describes your current role and you want the screen.

### Incentive structure

- Pay $200 for one completed 90-minute evaluation session.
- Pay a prorated $50 if the study team stops a scheduled session in the first 30 minutes for a reason unrelated to participant qualification or conduct.
- Offer an optional $75 for one 30-minute follow-up after the initial analysis; follow-up participation does not affect inclusion or preference scoring.
- Do not condition payment on task completion, positive feedback, product preference, pilot interest, or permission to quote the evaluator.
- Deliver compensation through the approved research-payment system after attendance is recorded; do not record payment handles or tax data here.

## Screening questionnaire

Record answers in the private recruiting system and write only the outcome in the log below.

1. **What is your current role, and which paid-social creative decisions do you personally own?** Disqualify if the candidate cannot name direct, current responsibility for briefing, production, approval, or launch.
2. **Is the brand you represent US-based, DTC, and actively selling through Shopify?** Disqualify for no US DTC brand, no Shopify storefront, or agency-only representation.
3. **What was the brand’s approximate Meta ad spend in the most recent complete month?** Use ranges; disqualify below $2,000, paused spend, or refusal to provide private verification.
4. **How many new paid-social creative concepts did your team launch in the last 30 days, and what was your part?** Disqualify if there is no current creative-throughput accountability.
5. **Describe the current path from product facts to approved Meta creative.** Classify the candidate as AI-tool, manual-workflow, or mixed; disqualify if the candidate cannot describe the working process.
6. **Which AI creative tools, if any, did you personally use in the last 60 days?** Record actual use; a trial without workflow use does not satisfy the AI-tool cohort bucket.
7. **If designers produce the work manually, how do briefs, revisions, approval, and handoff happen?** Use this to verify the manual-workflow bucket; disqualify vague or observer-only answers.
8. **Can you join a 90-minute remote session, share a sanitized brief, and attempt the workflow without coaching?** Disqualify if any required condition is declined.
9. **Do you have a friendship, family tie, employment, investment, active vendor negotiation, or product-development relationship with the founder or study team?** Disqualify any positive friend-of-founder or material-conflict response.
10. **Have you participated in another session for this product or received a detailed product walkthrough?** Disqualify repeat exposure from the core cohort; it may be recorded separately as non-scoring research.

## Evaluation-session protocol

1. Before the session, confirm qualification, conflict status, cohort bucket, consent, compensation terms, and the assigned sanitized brief. Do not disclose expected product outcomes.
2. Assign one primary golden brief for the unassisted usability measure. Across the cohort, distribute the remaining briefs for output review so every `GB-01` through `GB-20` brief is represented at least once and heavy-constraint briefs are spread across evaluators.
3. Give the evaluator a neutral start statement and the product entry point. The evaluator must complete **brief → quote → run → review** without coaching. The moderator may resolve a study-environment outage but must timestamp it; any task guidance, navigation cue, or interpretation counts as assistance.
4. Record completion, assistance, time to first reviewable static pack, quote comprehension, run-state comprehension, partial/failure handling, and whether lineage/cost evidence can be found.
5. Ask the evaluator to mark each reviewed job: no usable concept, one usable concept, or multiple usable concepts. “Usable” means the evaluator would advance the concept in the current workflow without rebuilding its core idea.
6. After hands-on work, compare against the evaluator’s current process on throughput, control, output quality, transparency, and expected cost. Capture a forced choice: prefer this workflow, prefer current workflow, or no preference, plus rationale.
7. Ask whether the evaluator would run a paid pilot under stated pricing. Interest is not paid proof; only an executed paid pilot or signed pilot counts toward that P0 gate.
8. Close with an open risk review covering unsupported claims, asset/likeness rights, brand control, hidden work, cost surprises, and reasons not to adopt. Do not sell or rebut.

Report results against the accepted P0 gates without changing denominators after observation:

- At least 80% of qualified evaluators complete the primary brief-to-review flow without assistance.
- At least 70% of representative jobs produce one or more evaluator-rated usable concepts.
- Median first reviewable static pack is at most 10 minutes and p90 is at most 15 minutes.
- At least three of the predeclared first five qualified completed evaluators prefer the workflow to their current process. Supplementary evaluators cannot replace a core negative result.
- Technical completion, landed cost, safety/trust failures, and paid-pilot proof are reconciled with system evidence; evaluator opinion cannot override those gates.
