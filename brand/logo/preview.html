Approved colour pairs

Three, and only three.

  • ink on paper
  • paper on ink
  • accent on the play shape only

In product UI use mark-mono.svg, not mark.svg. --signal is reserved for the one primary action on screen, and a blue logo would compete with it.

Clear space

Both lockups bake it in. The dashed rule marks the artwork bounds inside the file; everything outside it, up to the file edge, is clear space and ships as part of the asset.
