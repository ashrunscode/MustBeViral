# WashBodega trial briefs

Real-customer trial briefs replacing the synthetic golden briefs for the Phase E copy evaluation.

Every value below is sourced from a real artifact, not invented:

- **Site facts** — https://washbodega.com (scraped 2026-07-28): services, address, hours, phone,
  community program, Speed Queen history, FAQ answers.
- **Brand law** — `C:\dev\WashBodega\BRAND.md` (canonical brand sheet) and
  `C:\dev\WashBodega\tailwind.config.ts` (live site tokens).

**Why this is a harder test than the synthetic briefs.** WashBodega's brand sheet carries an
explicit hard-reject tone list (corporate-speak, coupon-y, childish, sterile, generic AI-sounding
copy, puns, fake luxury) and a claims-discipline rule that forbids inventing prices, ratings, or
amenities. Self-service prices are deliberately *not* published — they are posted in-store only. A
cheap model that hallucinates "$1.75/load" or writes a laundry pun fails on an objective, checkable
axis rather than on taste. That makes this slate separable without a subjective rubric alone.
