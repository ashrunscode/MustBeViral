# WashBodega trial briefs

Real-customer trial briefs replacing the synthetic golden briefs for the Phase E copy evaluation.

Every value below is sourced from a real artifact, not invented:

- **Site facts** — https://washbodega.com (scraped 2026-07-28): services, address, hours, phone,
  community program, Speed Queen history, FAQ answers.
- **Brand law** — `C:\dev\WashBodega\BRAND.md` (canonical brand sheet) and
  `C:\dev\WashBodega\tailwind.config.ts` (live site tokens).

**Why this is a harder test than the synthetic briefs.** WashBodega's brand sheet carries an
explicit hard-reject tone list (corporate-speak, coupon-y, childish, sterile, generic AI-sounding
copy, puns, fake luxury) and a claims-discipline rule that forbids inventing prices, ratings, or
amenities. A cheap model that writes a laundry pun, mixes up two price tiers, or drops a stated
minimum fails on an objective, checkable axis rather than on taste.

**Correction, 2026-07-29.** The first revision of these briefs asserted that prices were posted
in-store only and must never be stated. That was wrong: https://washbodega.com/pricing publishes
owner-confirmed prices, reviewed 2026-07-27. The briefs below now carry the real published prices,
which makes them a materially harder and more realistic test — the production risk is not a model
inventing a price out of nothing, it is a model quoting $1.09/lb for next-day Wash & Fold, dropping
the 15 lb minimum, or omitting that Wash & Go runs Friday-Sunday only. Prices are exact strings to
be reproduced, not facts to be paraphrased.
