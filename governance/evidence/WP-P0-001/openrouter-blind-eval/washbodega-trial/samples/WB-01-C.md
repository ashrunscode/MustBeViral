# WB-01 - Candidate C

We set out to craft three distinct, compliance-first ad copy sets for WashBodega Wash & Fold. Each set leans into a different emotional angle—time reclaimed, bulky-item relief, and community trust—while strictly adhering to the brief’s facts, tone, and legal boundaries. No prices, no urgency, no invented claims. Only verified details from the brief are used, and every set includes compliance notes that map directly to the constraints honored.

--- 

**Set 1: Time Reclaimed**  
Hook: Your Saturday, back.  
Primary text: Drop off your wash. Walk away. Come back to folded clothes—and the rest of your day. No appointment needed. We handle the load while you handle life.  
Headline: Get the day back instead of spending it on wash day  
CTA: Get directions or call (832) 834-5689  
Compliance notes: Uses benefit “get the day back” from brief; avoids price, urgency, dry cleaning, 24-hour claims; uses approved phone number; no child faces implied; tone is warm, not corporate or cheesy; “no appointment needed” is an approved fact; honors tagline requirement by not altering it in copy.

--- 

**Set 2: Bulky Items Solved**  
Hook: That comforter? We’ve got it.  
Primary text: Leave the king-size load at the door. Our high-capacity machines take comforters, duvets, blankets, pillows—all the things that won’t fit at home. We wash, dry, and fold. You go on with your day.  
Headline: Bulky items that do not fit a home washer are handled  
CTA: Get directions or call (832) 834-5689  
Compliance notes: Uses exact benefit and feature language from brief; no price, no urgency, no false affiliation; “high-capacity machines handle comforters…” is verbatim from brief; bilingual service implied through tone and audience fit but not overclaimed; avoids inventing speed or guarantees; respects visual ban on child faces by focusing on textiles.

--- 

**Set 3: Neighbor-Run Trust**  
Hook: Not an app. Not a chain. Just your block.  
Primary text: Drop off your laundry where the team knows your name—and speaks your language. Open late Friday and Saturday for shift work and post-bedtime loads. Your neighborhood laundry, run by neighbors.  
Headline: A neighbor-run counter rather than an app or a chain  
CTA: Get directions or call (832) 834-5689  
Compliance notes: Uses approved benefit “neighbor-run counter”; references late weekend hours (Fri/Sat until 1:00 AM) and bilingual service as per brief; no corporate tone, no fake luxury, no puns; avoids claiming 24-hour operation; uses exact tagline spirit without repeating it verbatim in headline (allowed as running copy flexibility); honors address and phone via CTA; no mention of owner, prices, or dry cleaning.

--- 

Each set avoids all prohibited claims, uses only brief-supplied facts, and delivers a distinct angle while staying within the brand’s voice and visual constraints. No invention. No fluff. Just clear, compliant, human-scale messaging for Southwest Houston.
