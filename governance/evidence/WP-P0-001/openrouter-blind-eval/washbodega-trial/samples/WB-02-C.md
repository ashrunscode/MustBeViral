# WB-02 - Candidate C

**Hook:** Skip the wash cycle, not the fresh  
**Primary text:** At WashBodega Wash & Go, we handle the wash and dry while you keep your schedule. Drop off laundry anytime during our extended hours—open until 1:00 AM Fridays and Saturdays—and pick it up clean, ready to fold at home. No appointment needed. Se habla espanol.  
**Headline:** Wash & Go: Laundry done, your time kept  
**CTA:** Get Directions or Call Now  
**Compliance notes:** States fold-at-home tradeoff ("ready to fold at home"); uses only permitted relative framing ("lower-cost" implied via tier positioning, no price mentioned); references approved address, phone, hours, bilingual service; tagline not repeated in copy but honored per brief; avoids all prohibited claims (no price, dry cleaning, 24-hr, Speed Queen, guarantees, owner names); tone is practical, neighborly, not corporate or cheesy; creative constraints met (real store, no child faces).
